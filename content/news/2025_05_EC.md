miXer, a new tool for CNV identification that we are developing, has been presented at European Human Genetics Conference (ESHG 2026) as poster "Accurate detection of clinical Copy Number Variants in exomes with X chromosome reader-based Machine Learning", in collaboration with Tommaso Pippucci (Sant’Orsola Bologna) and Paola Dimartino (UniPV).
