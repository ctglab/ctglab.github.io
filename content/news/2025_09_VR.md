Our lab took part in [Bioinformatiha 12](http://www.bioinformatiha.it/index.php?pg=bh12), the Tuscan Day of Bioinformatics and Systems Biology held at the Fondazione Pisana per la Scienza, with two oral presentations. Danilo talked about "Extending tumor-only RNA neoantigens identification in Merkel Cell Carcinoma", while Valeria presented "Quantum Kernels for Breast Cancer Stratification: Exploring Expressivity on Real-World Multi-Omics Data".
